Outreach

Emails go out only after you approve them in a batch (up to 30). They're sent from separate sending inboxes, never from support@, within each inbox's hourly and daily limits, 2–6 minutes apart, during the recipient's business hours, with at most 2 follow-ups. Replies stop follow-ups. Opt-outs, bounces and complaints are suppressed automatically, and an inbox pauses itself if bounces or complaints rise.
