      How a platform moves through the process

      1. Discover & evaluateAgent finds the site and checks it is legitimate, relevant and free.
      2. Approve the listingYou approve the drafted text (skipped when auto-approval is on).
      3. Sign up & submitAgent creates the account, verifies email and fills the form.
      4. Platform reviewThe platform moderates the submission. Can take days or weeks.
      5. PublishedA public page shows the product. Agent finds it and checks the link.
      6. Backlink liveLink to the site verified on an indexable page. Done; re-checked daily.

      At any step the agent may stop for you (Needs you) or a step may fail. Only steps 5–6 count as Successfully added; step 4 counts as submitted.
